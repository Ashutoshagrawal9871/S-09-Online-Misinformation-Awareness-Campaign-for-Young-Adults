## 2. Masterclass 1: "Digital Forensics & The SIFT Framework"

- **Target Audience:** General undergraduate & postgraduate student cohort
- **Format:** Hybrid (Interactive Campus Auditorium + Zoom Livestream)

| Time | Module | Key Learning Objectives | Activity / Materials |

| **00:30 - 00:55** | **Hands-on Lab 1: Image & Video Verification** | Learn Google Lens, TinEye, and InVID Keyframe extraction. | Breakout groups analyze 3 recycled disaster photos. |

## 4. Breakout Group Exercises & Case Study Cards

### Case Study A: The Viral Medical Miracle
- **The Claim:** *"AIIMS doctors confirm that sniffing camphor and clove oil immediately restores oxygen saturation and cures viral infections."*
- **The Task:**
  1. Identify primary keywords.
  2. Search peer-reviewed databases (PubMed) and fact-checking registries (BOOM Live, PIB Fact Check).
  3. Formulate a 2-sentence polite WhatsApp debunking card.

### Case Study B: The Recycled Disaster Footage
- **The Claim:** *"Breaking: Massive cyclone hits coastal university campus today (attached dramatic flood video)!"*
- **The Task:**
  1. Take video screenshots of keyframes.
  2. Perform reverse image search on Yandex and Google Lens.
  3. Discover original footage recorded during a 2017 flood in a different country.
  4. Write a timestamped verification report.

## 5. Facilitator Troubleshooting & FAQs

### Q: "What if an older relative refuses to accept the fact-check?"
> **Facilitator Answer:** "Do not attack the person's intelligence or beliefs. Frame the response around protecting them: *'I love you and want to make sure no scammer tricks you with this fake link.'* Share the official link rather than arguing."

### Q: "Can AI detection tools (like Hive or GPTZero) be trusted 100%?"
> **Facilitator Answer:** "No. Automated AI detectors have false positive and negative rates. Always triangulate automated tools with lateral reading, corroborating reporting, and provenance tracking."

## 6. Peer Multiplier Responsibilities & Code of Conduct
1. **Act as Campus Fact-Checking First Responders:** Clarify viral student rumors within batch channels.
2. **Promote the SIFT Method:** Always explain *how* a claim was debunked rather than just asserting "it's fake".
3. **Maintain Non-Partisanship:** Adhere to the International Fact-Checking Network (IFCN) code of principles.
