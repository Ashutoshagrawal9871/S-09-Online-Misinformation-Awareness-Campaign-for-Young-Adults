## 2. Joint Masterclass Curriculum: "Frontline Fact-Checking & OSINT Investigations"

## 4. Live Newsroom Simulation Briefs

### Simulation Case 1: Geolocation & Event Verification
- **Scenario:** A viral 15-second video circulating on Telegram claims a major chemical explosion occurred 1 hour ago in an industrial zone near the city.
- **Student Objective:**
  1. Extract keyframes using InVID.
  2. Identify landmarks (billboards, street signs, building facade architecture).
  3. Use Google Maps Street View and reverse image search to discover the clip was recorded during a fire in Beirut 4 years ago.
  4. Publish a 3-point debunk bulletin.

### Simulation Case 2: Deepfake Audio Impersonation
- **Scenario:** An audio recording surfaces on WhatsApp where a university dean allegedly announces the immediate suspension of semester examinations.
- **Student Objective:**
  1. Perform spectral frequency analysis (Audacity / HuggingFace deepfake audio detector).
  2. Identify robotic voice artifacts and lack of natural ambient background room tone.
  3. Contact official university PR office to obtain authoritative refutation.
  4. Draft official student council clarification.

## 5. Ongoing Partnership Continuity Model
1. **Student Fact-Checking Internship Pipeline:** Top-performing peer multipliers are recommended for summer editorial internships with partner fact-checking organizations.
2. **Direct Tipline Integration:** Campus fact-checking desk routes complex viral regional claims to partner investigative teams via priority WhatsApp tiplines.
3. **Annual Media Literacy Symposium:** Jointly organized annual panel on digital citizenship and AI ethics.
