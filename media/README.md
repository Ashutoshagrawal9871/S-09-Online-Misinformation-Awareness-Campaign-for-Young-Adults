## What belongs here

| Type | Examples |
| :--- | :--- |
| Photographs | Campus outreach tables, workshop rooms, poster displays |
| Videos | Short clips of sessions, student interviews (with consent) |
| Records | Scanned questionnaires, interview summaries, attendance sheets |
| Permissions | Approval letters or acknowledgements from partner institutions |

## Privacy rules

- Obtain informed consent before photographing or recording participants.
- Do not publish identifiable student data, emails, phone numbers, or signatures.
- Keep original consent forms outside the public repository; store only anonymised summaries here.
- Avoid uploading large raw video files to Git. Link to the official submission drive instead and keep only a manifest/contact sheet here.

## Current status

The public demo focuses on the software platform and campaign documents. Field evidence is submitted through the CUSoC designated drive, not committed to this public repository, to protect participant privacy. Add a `FIELD_REPORT.md` or contact sheet here if your evaluation requires the evidence to live in the repo.
