## 6. Key Learnings & Amplification Strategy
1. **Short-Form Video Drives Immediate Curiosity:** Short, punchy reels highlighting visual glitches in AI videos generated the highest inbound traffic to the web platform.
2. **Actionable WhatsApp Copy-Paste Templates Solve Friction:** Providing ready-to-use polite debunking texts removed the social awkwardness students felt when correcting misinformation in family chats.
3. **Interactive Simulation Boosts Retention:** Users who tested claims using the live SIFT engine had an 82% higher retention rate than static PDF readers.
