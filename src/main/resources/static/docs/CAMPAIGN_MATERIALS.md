## 2. Digital Infographic Posters

### Poster 1: The SIFT Framework (Pocket Verification Guide)
**Headline:** *Before You Share, Run the SIFT Test!*  
**Visual Style:** High-contrast Indigo & Emerald palette, 4-step vertical flow with iconography.

| Step | Action | Practical Guidance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **S** | **STOP** | Check your emotional state. Did this post make you furious, terrified, or ecstatic? Misinformation targets emotions to bypass critical thinking. |
| **I** | **INVESTIGATE the Source** | Who published this? Check domain URL (`.gov.in` vs `.gov.co.in.fake`), author credentials, and "About Us" page. |
| **F** | **FIND Better Coverage** | Open a new tab. Has BOOM Live, Alt News, Snopes, or Reuters reported this? Look for trusted consensus. |
| **T** | **TRACE Claims & Media** | Find original context. Right-click images to Reverse Search on Google Lens / TinEye. Search original quotes. |

**Footer Call-to-Action:**  
*Scan the QR Code to verify any claim with the TruthLens SIFT Analyzer at truthlens.university.edu*

---

### Poster 2: Spot the Deepfake & AI Hallucination
**Headline:** *Don't Believe Everything You See or Hear*  
**Visual Breakdown:**
1. **Facial Glitches:** Inspect earlobes, teeth alignment, irregular eye blinks, and unnatural skin smoothing.
2. **Audio Sync Inconsistencies:** Watch lip movement synchronization against rapid plosive sounds ('P', 'B', 'M').
3. **Background Warping:** Look for warped architecture, duplicate audience members, or nonsensical background text.
4. **Context Check:** Did any legitimate press corps record this speech?

## 3. WhatsApp "Rumor Radar" Anti-Misinformation Response Pack
When friends or family members share viral fake forwards in WhatsApp groups, use these polite, non-confrontational scripted replies with verified citations.

### Template A: De-escalating Health Myth Forwards
```
Hey everyone! Just checked this claim about [Garlic/Hot Water/Herbal Remedy] curing [Disease]. 
While home remedies can be soothing, medical authorities (WHO / AIIMS) confirmed this does not cure the condition and delaying real treatment can be dangerous.

Here is the fact-check breakdown:
👉 https://www.boomlive.in/health-check
Let's keep our family healthy and safe from unverified medical forwards! ❤️
```

### Template B: Countering Financial / Phishing Lures
```
⚠️ Warning: This link offering "Free Rs 5,000 / Free Laptops" from the Government is a known phishing scam.
The official government domain is always .gov.in or .nic.in, not bit.ly or .top.
Entering your details on this link can compromise your bank accounts and passwords.

Verified update from PIB Fact Check:
👉 https://factcheck.pib.gov.in/
Please do not click or forward!
```

### Template C: Countering Communal or Sensational Hate Speech / Fake Videos
```
Hi guys, this video is actually from a 2018 event in a different state and is being shared with a misleading caption to cause panic.
A quick reverse image search shows the original source here:
👉 https://www.altnews.in/verified-report
Let's pause and verify before forwarding sensitive videos that create tension. 🙏
```
